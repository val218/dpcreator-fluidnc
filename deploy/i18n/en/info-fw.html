dpCREATOR FluidNC is FluidNC 4.1.1 with the things a working machine needs: it picks a job up again after a power cut, lets you change the depth while it cuts, and talks to the SmartTabUI pendant and the dpCREATOR apps. It is free for registered customers and runs any FluidNC config.yaml.

Flash it in your browserRelease notes

What it adds to FluidNC

Job recovery

The board writes down where every SD-card job is. After a power cut or an E-stop it suggests the safest line to restart from, lets you dry-run to it, and carries on.

How job recovery works

Live Z adjust and rewind

Change the cutting depth while the job runs, or hold, wind the tool back along the path with the handwheel and re-cut.

Live Z adjust and handwheel rewind

Wired Ethernet

On the R2 and R3, a W5500 Ethernet connection to your network instead of Wi-Fi.

Ethernet (W5500) setup

SD card on the network

Open the controller's SD card as a network drive and copy jobs to it from your computer.

SD card as a network drive

A login for the board

Protect the board on your network with a password. Set it when you install, or on your first visit.

Set up on the Flash Firmware page

Pendant and apps

Works with the SmartTabUI pendant (measure, Record & Retrace, job recovery at the machine) and runs the dpCREATOR apps from the controller's own memory.

System overview

Which build for your board

BoardBuildWhat you get
dpCREATOR R3 and R2ESP32-S3Everything above, with W5500 Ethernet. The full WebUI is free with the R3.
Other ESP32-S3 boardsESP32-S3The same build. Needs an ESP32-S3 module with 8 MB PSRAM.
ESP32 (WROOM-32) boardsESP32Wi-Fi instead of Ethernet; features that need more memory use smaller versions. WebUI Lite is free.

Install and update

  1. Log in to your dpcreator.eu account (free).
  2. Open the Flash Firmware page in Chrome or Edge on a computer and connect the board by USB.
  3. Pick your board and a configuration, tick the apps, enter your Wi-Fi and a login, and press Flash. Nothing is installed on your computer.
