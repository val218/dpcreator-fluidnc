Built for the shop floor.

Connect everything. SD card, wired Ethernet and USB on the board, plus RS485, UART, relay, 0–10 V and PWM on clearly labelled terminals.

Stop means stop. Without the E-stop circuit closed, no step or direction signal leaves the board. A safety you can trust because it isn't software.

Wire it once. Each axis has its own block: LIMIT COM, LIMIT, GND, DIR 5 V, GND, STEP 5 V for external drivers. Switches and NPN sensors plug straight in.
