Before the official launch we are inviting 10 makers to build the TabUi pendant and run the dpCREATOR WebUI on their own machines, then tell us honestly what works and what doesn't. Your build, photos and report shape the product before anyone else buys it.

What you get

TabUi firmware licenceFree, for your pendant.
TabUi case 3D filesThe PETG print files for the enclosure.
WebUI licenceFree, for your controller.
Free R3 controllerFor the 3 best builds out of 10.

You keep the licences after the programme, with the same free updates as any customer.

What we ask

  • Build the pendant and install the WebUI within 8 weeks of receiving your licences.
  • Send a report using the template below, with at least 5 photos (parts, wiring, finished pendant, machine) and a short video of jogging and running a job.
  • Share problems and ideas in the private tester group as you go.
  • Publishing about it publicly is welcome but optional. If you do, say that you received the licences free as part of the R&D Club.

Who we are looking for

A mix of machines: CNC routers, lasers, plotters and drag knives, plasma, and retrofits of older GRBL machines. A mix of people: first-time FluidNC users as well as experienced ones. Makers who already share their builds on YouTube, Instagram or forums are especially welcome. Places are for the EU and UK.

What you need

  • A free dpcreator.eu account. The licences are delivered to it.
  • Your own pendant parts: the JC2432W328C display, 60 mm MPG handwheel, E-stop, rotary switches, PCF8574 module and the rest of the parts list, plus a 3D printer for PETG.
  • A controller: a dpCREATOR R2 or R3, or another ESP32-S3 board with 16 MB flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM (N16R8).
  • A machine to run it on, and soldering tools. The build is demanding: plan up to four hours.

How it works

  1. Register an account and apply with the form below.
  2. We pick 10 testers and email everyone, picked or not.
  3. Testers get a personal code: add the TabUi firmware, case files and WebUI to the cart, enter the code, and check out at €0. The licences and downloads appear in your account.
  4. Build, test, and send your report within 8 weeks.
  5. We pick the 3 best builds and send each one a dpCREATOR R3 controller.

How the 3 best builds are chosen: how complete and useful the report is (problems found, unclear steps in the guides, ideas), the quality of the photos and video, and the quality of the build itself. dpCREATOR decides, and the decision is final.

Report template

  1. You and your machine: machine type and size, drivers, spindle or laser, controller board.
  2. Build: how long it took, where you got stuck, anything unclear or wrong in the guide.
  3. Setup: flashing, config.yaml, WebUI install, connecting the pendant.
  4. Use: jogging, running jobs, E-stop and job recovery, Z adjust. What worked, what didn't.
  5. Problems: each bug with what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Logs or screenshots if you have them.
  6. Ideas: what would make it better.
  7. Verdict: a rating from 1 to 10 and an honest comment in a few sentences.
  8. Media: at least 5 photos and a short video (links are fine).
