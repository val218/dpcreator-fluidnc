Build your own machine one part at a time: add each motor, sensor and output as you wire it, test it, then teach the positions and run the sequence from the controller. Motion writes the controller config and the G-code for you.

Install Motion on your controller

Free with the R3 €39 for other boards Any FluidNC 4.x

Motion is copied into the controller's flash, next to its WebUI. It then works without internet, reads the controller's config, and runs your sequences from the controller itself.

  • dpCREATOR firmware: opens at http://<your board>/motion.html, with live inputs and outputs, fast sensor waits and the step names on the SmartTabUI pendant.
  • Stock FluidNC 4.x: opens at http://<your board>/flash/motion.html. Sequences run the same way; limit switches and the probe show live.

Needs FluidNC 4.0 or newer (stock or dpCREATOR) and about 400 KB free in the flash. The installer checks the firmware, that the machine is idle and the free space before it writes anything.
