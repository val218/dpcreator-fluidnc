This guide takes you from the parts on the bench to a working TabUi pendant. It is written for the DIY pack (firmware licence and case print files) and for anyone who builds the pendant from their own parts. Plan for about two hours, plus the time the silicone needs to dry.

Tools: soldering iron, side cutters, wire stripper, Phillips screwdriver, multimeter, USB-C cable.

1. Remove the RGB LED from the display board

The JC2432W328C has an RGB LED on the front, next to the display. It is wired to IO4, IO16 and IO17: the same pins the pendant uses on connector P6 for the handwheel and the E-stop. Left in place, it loads those lines and the inputs don't read reliably. Desolder it with a soldering iron or hot air.

2. Assemble the front panel

  1. Display. Peel the protective film off the display first. Place the board in the front panel and fix it with the four M3 × 5 mm self-tapping screws.
    Be careful with the display connector as the board goes into the printed panel: it is a tight fit there.
    Then run a bead of 704 silicone around the perimeter of the board, wherever it can reach the case. Press the display down while the silicone dries and make sure it stays fully seated.
  2. Handwheel. Fit the 60 mm MPG through the round opening and tighten it on its three mounting studs.
  3. STEP selector. Fit the 3-position rotary switch (RS16 or RS26) in the left opening.
  4. AXIS selector. Fit the 5-position rotary switch in the right opening.
If your rotary switches have an adjustable stop washer, set it to 3 positions for STEP and 5 for AXIS before you tighten the nut, so the knob stops at x1 / x100 and at OFF / A.

3. Assemble the main case

  1. Screw the E-stop button (16 mm) into the left side of the case.
  2. Screw the ENABLE button (12 mm) into the right side.
  3. Leave the GX12 connector out for now. It goes in after the wiring, so the wires are easy to reach.

4. Wire it

Everything connects to the connectors on the back of the display board: P1 (serial and power), P6 (handwheel and E-stop), CN1 (I²C to the PCF8574) and P4 (speaker). Nothing is soldered to the board itself.

  1. Fix the PCF8574 module to the back of the display board with a blob of 704 silicone, clear of the ESP32 module.
  2. Plug the speaker into P4 (SPEAK) and fix it inside the case with a dab of silicone.
  3. Put a dab of silicone on the solder joints and connector plugs so they can't work loose.
  4. Fit the GX12 connector in the bottom of the case and solder its five wires.

5. Close the case

Lay the wires flat so nothing is pinched, place the front panel on the case and fix it with the 7 black M3 screws. Fit the knobs on the STEP and AXIS switches so the pointer lines up with x1 and OFF.

6. Flash and test

  1. Flash the pendant firmware over USB-C from Firmware & Downloads. Unplug the controller cable while you flash: the serial pins are shared.
  2. Hold the ENABLE button during the first second of boot to open the pendant settings, then tap Inputs.
  3. The Input Monitor shows every input live. Turn both selectors through every position, press ENABLE and the E-stop, and turn the handwheel: each one must change its line on the screen. Hold the E-stop for 2 seconds to leave.

When every input checks out, connect the pendant to the controller: see Connecting the TabUi pendant.
