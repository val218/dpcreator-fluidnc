What changed in each build of the dpCREATOR controller firmware, the SmartTabUI pendant firmware and the dpCREATOR WebUI, newest first. All three update from the Flash Firmware page. They are developed together, so update all of them at the same time.

Found a bug? Write to us with what you did, what happened and the build numbers: the WebUI footer shows the WebUI and controller builds, and the pendant shows its build on the boot screen. If the controller restarted by itself, add the crash report: $Crash in the console, or Copy details in the WebUI.

dpCREATOR controller firmware

FluidNC 4.1.1 with W5500 Ethernet and the dpCREATOR commands. Free for every customer.

17 October 2026

  • New: the SmartTabUI pendant reports its chip ID and build to the controller when it connects, so the WebUI can show which pendant is attached.
  • Changed: the default config.yaml and the docs name the SmartTabUI pendant.

16 October 2026

  • New: the Record & Retrace waypoint list is kept on the board's SD card. The pendant and the WebUI edit the same list, and the pendant works with it without a PC. Saving writes /retrace.nc with drill holes and round or rectangle pockets.
  • New: when a WebUI tool (Centre Finder, Offcuts) takes the pendant's double-taps, the pendant is told, so the point goes to the right place.

15 October 2026

  • New: a double-tap on the pendant's ENABLE button sends a point to the WebUI tools.

13 October 2026

  • New: the board's 8 MB PSRAM is used. Boards with a module that has too little PSRAM get a warning in the WebUI and on the Flash Firmware page.
  • New: the board reports its ID, which the WebUI installer uses. The dpCREATOR WebUI is now installed separately from the firmware.
  • Fixed: the controller could crash on SD card access when its internal memory ran low. Pendant previews now wait for memory instead.

12 October 2026

  • Fixed: restarts by the task watchdog. Opening the WebUI could lock up the controller while it was sending status reports.
  • Fixed: the WebUI became slow, and pendant previews failed, while the controller was sending to the pendant. Sends to the pendant are buffered and previews are paced.
  • Improved: the WebUI stays connected while jogging. Old status reports are dropped instead of the connection being closed.
  • New: after a watchdog restart the controller keeps a report of what it was doing, shown by $Crash, in the boot log and in the WebUI.

SmartTabUI pendant firmware

The pendant firmware, installed from the Flash Firmware page. Your pendant settings are kept on update.

17 October 2026

  • New: registration. A pendant is registered by its chip ID the first time it is installed with a licence. Nothing is locked: an unregistered pendant works fully and shows Not registered on the boot screen and in Settings. See Installing, updating and registering SmartTabUI.
  • New: Record mode can set the feed, plunge and ramp (helix entry angle) for each waypoint.
  • New: Rewind shows a progress bar of how far back along the path it is, and the panel stays open until Rewind ends.
  • Changed: Measure → Go to centre lifts Z to the top first, like Home → Go to 0.

16 October 2026

  • New firmware: SmartTabUI, written from scratch. DRO with the step selector, home and probe menu, macros, spindle, file browser with toolpath preview, Prepare and Run, live Z adjust, Rewind, job recovery and Record & Retrace.
  • New: a double-tap on ENABLE asks whether to measure the part or record waypoints.
  • New: Dark, Neutral and Light themes; Settings with an input monitor and a UART monitor; restart with E-stop + ENABLE held for 3 seconds.
  • New: boot screen with the build number, and beeps when you turn the axis or step selector.
  • Fixed: the Record & Retrace popup closed by itself after each point.
  • Fixed: live Z adjust now stays on screen until the controller confirms it.
  • Fixed: running a file no longer holds up other commands from the pendant.

dpCREATOR WebUI

The browser interface, installed on the board from the Flash Firmware page (WebUI tab).

17 October 2026

  • New: update notice. About once a day the WebUI asks dpcreator.eu whether newer builds exist and shows update available in its footer. You can turn this off in the footer.
  • New: the footer shows the build of the connected pendant.
  • New: registration notice. Nothing is locked: an unregistered WebUI shows a small notice that you can hide for a week.

16 October 2026

  • New: Record & Retrace has a waypoint map and an editor for the selected waypoint, matching Record mode on the pendant.
  • New: the waypoint list is kept on the board and shared with the pendant; rectangle pockets.
  • New: Measure part on the pendant opens the Centre Finder; clearing the corners clears them on both.

15 October 2026

  • New: Centre Finder: measure a part from its corners, see its size, set work zero at its centre or a corner, and make a surfacing job.
  • New: Record & Retrace: jog to marked points, add waypoints and turn them into a drilling or pocketing job.
  • New: Offcuts → DXF: record the outlines of offcuts or the workpiece (with 45° edge snap) and export them as a DXF to place your drawing on.
  • New: tool library with a floating tool picker, tool-change modes with park positions per tool, and an M6 macro generator.
  • New: probe the material height, set Z0 from the tool setter, and set X0/Y0 at measured points.
  • New: jogging (including 45° diagonals) keeps working while Centre Finder, Record & Retrace or Offcuts are open.
  • Fixed: files saved from the WebUI could arrive on the board empty. Saves are now checked and retried.
  • Fixed: the Record & Retrace list was squeezed by the G-code preview and could add the same point twice.
  • Fixed: the Record & Retrace window could stay on screen after closing; safe Z now defaults to 5 mm and jobs with problems are blocked.

13 October 2026

  • New: the dpCREATOR WebUI is a separate product, installed on the board from dpcreator.eu.
  • Fixed: two WebUI tabs open at once no longer fight over the connection to the board.
  • New: a warning when the board's module has no 8 MB PSRAM, and a message explaining when pendant previews pause because the board is low on memory.
