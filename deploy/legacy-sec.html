Where the R2 and R3 come from

Each dpCREATOR board was built on what the one before taught us. The earlier boards are discontinued, but owners can still identify theirs and find notes on the legacy page.

  1. v3.0.2 (v1)

    ESP32 dev module, plug-in

    A carrier board for a standard ESP32 dev module, plugged in rather than soldered.

  2. R1 (v4.0.2)

    ESP32 on board, FluidNC

    The first R-series board: microSD, relay, USB and four step/direction axes, in a DIN-rail case.

  3. v4.0.4 (modular)

    Plug-in modules, FluidNC

    Core, motion control, 0–10 V spindle and power supply as separate modules, each replaceable.

  4. R2 and R3

    ESP32-S3, W5500 Ethernet, today

    Wired Ethernet, the TabUi pendant port and 16 MB flash with 8 MB PSRAM.

Before the ESP32 boards there were two GRBL 1.1 boards for the Arduino UNO, made by 3dp.lv: the 3 Axis CNC Shield V1.2 and the UNO R3 CNC Edition.
